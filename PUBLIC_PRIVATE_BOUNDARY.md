# Public research and private operations boundary

## Public repository purpose

Tallystick is the inspectable research workbench for sports-fact provenance, correction replay and market-rule checks. Public material may include original code, formal or executable specifications, fictional matches, synthetic source roles, conformance tests, bounded models and reproducible evidence bundles.

The public repository is not a sportsbook, wagering service, licensed live-data feed, customer account system or production settlement service.

## Material that may remain public

- fictional match and market fixtures;
- generic provenance, completeness and correction rules;
- open-source verification code and public schemas;
- research calculations, bounded models and conformance tests;
- non-confidential interoperability documentation.

## Material that must remain private

- licensed or contract-restricted live sports data;
- credentials, signing keys, feed tokens and production infrastructure;
- customer, bettor, trader, account, payment or transaction data;
- real market positions, risk limits, commercial pricing and settlement instructions;
- supplier contracts, private compliance reviews and security incidents;
- non-public league, regulator, partner or customer configurations.

## Production split trigger

Create a private operational repository or workspace when the project first handles a real data licence, live feed, customer configuration, market exposure, payment integration or production settlement process. The public verifier and schema may remain here while adapters, secrets, real data and commercial configuration stay private.

Do not use public issue discussions for licensed data samples, real transactions or confidential legal advice. Synthetic reproductions should preserve the technical problem without exposing restricted material.

## Release check

Before publication, establish publication rights, inspect changes and generated artifacts for secrets and personal or licensed data, verify all examples are fictional or cleared, and limit claims to the tested implementation. Removing an artifact from the current branch does not by itself remove it from Git history.

The repository's Apache-2.0 and CC BY 4.0 notices apply only to the material they identify. Public visibility creates no authority to operate a market, use a third-party feed or process real wagers.
